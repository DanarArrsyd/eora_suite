# EORA brand mark — "Stack"

**Status:** chosen by the owner on 2026-11-12 (concept C of three). Draft brand asset; not yet used in the product UI.

## Meaning
Three stacked plates seen from above — the way sheet and coil become parts, and parts become a finished product:

- **bottom plate (navy):** material — coil and sheet as received, the ledger that holds it;
- **middle plate (blue):** production — material turned into parts at each stage;
- **top plate (bright blue, lit face):** the finished product and the decision made on it.

The stack also reads as layers of one platform (planning, materials, production, quality, delivery) on a single ledger, and as a modular SaaS. Nothing in it belongs to a customer, an industry segment or a country.

The name is written in lower case — **eora** — as a calm, product-first wordmark. A proposed reading of the letters, for presentations only: **E**nterprise **O**perations, **R**esources & **A**nalytics.

## Use
- Clear space around the mark: at least the height of one plate on every side.
- Smallest size: 16 px for the app icon, 20 px for the bare mark.
- Do not recolour the plates individually, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, or stretch the mark.

## Before public use
"Eora" is also the name of the Aboriginal people of the Sydney area in Australia. Before registering or launching the brand publicly, check the trademark register in the target markets and consider how the name is perceived there.
